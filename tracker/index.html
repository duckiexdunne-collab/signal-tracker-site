A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Honest progress.
No mystery percentages.

This is a development tracker, not a promise that every feature is already in the game. A plan describes an idea. A vector draft describes proposed geometry. A compiled build still needs a look and a walk around in DayZ.

Needs evidence means the audit couldn’t verify the outcome. Blocked means there’s a known issue or approval gate. Approved sample covers only the particular version and location recorded.

Internal files, raw logs and personal notes stay private. The public board shares the useful status, acceptance criteria and next steps. The audit date tells you when those claims were checked.
